# Challenge

Add Challenge to your group and do the thing together: steps, workouts, no sugar, reading, or any daily habit.

- **One clear rule**: it helps you settle what counts as a day done, and how long to run it.
- **Check in by saying so**: "done" or "9k steps" in the chat, or one tap in the Leaderboard.
- **Streaks and a leaderboard**: totals, current streaks, and who's in today.
- **A nudge each evening**: a cheer for who's in and a word for the rest. Nobody gets named for missing.
- **Milestones**: a shout at 7, 14 and 30 days, and for coming back after a break.

It cheers for everyone the same. It isn't a doctor or a coach: for health questions, ask a professional.
