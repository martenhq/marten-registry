# A challenge people finish

## One rule, said in a line

- A day is done or it isn't. "10,000 steps", "a workout of 20 minutes or more", "no added sugar", "20 pages".
- No points, no tiers, no exceptions list. If people want levels, let each person pick their own target at the start
  and count "hit my target" as the rule.
- Write it down where everyone sees it: the chat's rules and the Leaderboard's settings.

## The right size

- **Length**: 21 or 30 days for a habit, 7 to 14 for a first try or a busy month. Longer than 30 loses most groups;
  offer a second round instead.
- **Level**: something everyone can do on a bad day. The person with the least time sets the floor; the keen ones can
  do more, it still counts once.
- **Start**: a Monday, or the first of the month, two or three days out, so people can get ready.

## Rest and catch-ups

- For training, build in rest: "5 days a week" or one free day a week, so nobody hurts themselves for a streak.
- Allow logging a day late (by the next evening). People forget to say; they didn't skip.
- A missed day breaks the streak, not the challenge. The total still counts, and the next day starts a new streak.

## What to celebrate

- Streak milestones: 3, 7, 14, 21, 30 days.
- A personal best streak, and coming back after a break.
- The group: "everyone checked in today" is worth a line.
- Never celebrate by comparing someone with someone below them.

## Keep it light

- One check-in nudge a day, in the evening, about the group, never naming who hasn't.
- If the group goes quiet for three days, ask once whether to change the rule, pause, or stop. Ending early is fine.

## Know the limits

Habits and motivation only. Anything about pain, injury, food restrictions, pregnancy or a health condition: say to
check with a doctor first, and don't suggest targets for them.
