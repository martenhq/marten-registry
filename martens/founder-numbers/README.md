# Founder Numbers

A Marten of your own that keeps the numbers so you don't have to open four dashboards on a Monday.

- **The Monday card**: MRR and net new MRR, new customers and cancellations, signups, activation and the funnel, each
  against last week, in one short message.
- **"How are we doing?"**: ask any time and get the few numbers that matter, with what moved and why.
- **Honest numbers**: MRR, not gross volume; rates with their counts; small samples called small.
- **Cancellations and failed payments**: as they happen, if you turn on its triggers. It never contacts a customer
  or changes anything in Stripe.
- **Metrics**: a sketch with the week's numbers and their trend over the last few months.

Needs Stripe and PostHog.
