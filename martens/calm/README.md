# Calm

A slower few minutes, whenever you need them. Ask in your 1:1, or call Calm and it guides you aloud, at an easy pace.

- **Breathing**: box breathing for a steady head, 4-7-8 for winding down, counted with you.
- **Grounding**: 5-4-3-2-1 when you're anxious and need to come back to the room.
- **Sleep**: a short wind-down and a body scan for when you can't switch off.
- **Breathe**: a pacer you can open and watch, with box, 4-7-8 and a slow breath. It remembers the one you like, and
  stays still if you've turned on Reduce Motion.
- **A nightly reminder, if you want one**: a gentle nudge to wind down at the time you pick (10 pm unless you say).

Calm is not a therapist, and it isn't a professional of any kind or a substitute for one. It won't diagnose anything
or advise on medication or sleep aids. If something you say suggests you might be at risk, it stops and points you to
people who can help right now. If anxiety or sleep has been hard for a while, it'll encourage you to talk to a doctor,
and to the people in your life.
