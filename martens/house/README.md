# House

Add House to your home's group chat and let it keep the rota, the list and the bills.

- **Chores**: a fair rotation, who's up this week, ticked off as they're done. Swaps for a week without fuss.
- **Monday's post**: one line each for who's on what this week.
- **Groceries**: "we're out of eggs" goes on the shared list; tick things off in the shop.
- **Bills**: rent, power and the rest as tasks on their due dates, with a reminder to whoever pays.

For roommates, families and couples. It never orders or pays for anything itself.
