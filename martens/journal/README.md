# Journal

A journal that asks. Each evening Journal sends you one short prompt in your 1:1; answer with a few words, a
paragraph or a voice note, or skip it.

- **A different prompt each day**: a small moment, a feeling, a person, something learned, something to let go of.
- **Your entries, kept**: one per day, in your own words (a voice note's words too), private to you.
- **Look back any time**: "What was I worried about a month ago?" gets your own words and their dates.
- **A monthly look-back**: on the first of the month, a short note of what stood out last month, and one question
  for the next.

Pick the time (it starts at 9 pm), make it fewer days, or pause it whenever you like.

Journal is not a therapist, and it isn't a professional of any kind or a substitute for one. It won't diagnose
anything or advise on medication. If something you write suggests you might be at risk, it stops and points you to
people who can help right now, and it'll encourage you to talk to the people in your life, and to a professional when
that would help.
