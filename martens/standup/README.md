# Standup

Add Standup to your team's group and drop the daily meeting.

- **Each weekday morning**: it asks what you did, what you're doing, and what's in your way.
- **Answer however you like**: a sentence, bullets or a voice note, whenever suits you.
- **One short summary**: posted late morning, readable in ten seconds.
- **Blockers don't get lost**: each one becomes a task for whoever's blocked, and stays on the Board until it's cleared.
- **The Board**: everyone's update for the day, and every open blocker.

It works for the whole team, takes no sides, and never reports on anyone.
