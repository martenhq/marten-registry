# Trip Planner

Add Trip Planner to your group and plan the trip where you're already talking about it.

- **Dates**: it gathers what works for everyone and puts the options to a vote.
- **Itinerary**: a day-by-day plan everyone can open, kept up to date as you decide.
- **Bookings**: "I'll book the hotel" becomes your task, with a nudge before it's due.
- **Budget**: every cost, who paid, the total and each person's share; who owes whom at the end.
- **The day before**: a short run-down of when to meet and what to bring.

Works on its own for a solo trip too. Connect Gmail and it can find your flight and hotel confirmations.
