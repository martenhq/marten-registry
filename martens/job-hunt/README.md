# Job Hunt

Your job search, kept in one place.

- **Pipeline**: every application on a board, applied, interviewing, offer or closed, with the next step on each.
- **Follow-ups**: it sets its own reminders to chase a quiet application or send a thank-you, and drafts them for you.
- **Interview prep**: your stories in STAR shape, notes on the company, and good questions to ask.
- **Mock interviews**: call it and it plays the interviewer, then tells you what went well and what to work on.
- **Mondays**: a short look at what's moving and what's due.

Connect Gmail and it spots replies from companies and updates the board. It never sends or replies to an email on its own.
