# Reflect

Someone to talk things through with, whenever you need to: in your 1:1, by text, a voice note or a call.

- **Listens first**: it lets you say it your way, reflects back what it heard, and asks one good question at a time.
- **Doesn't rush to fix**: it asks before it offers ideas, and when it does, they're options, not answers.
- **Notices patterns**: it remembers across weeks and gently points out what keeps coming up, as a question you can
  wave away.
- **Sums up**: at the end of a talk, two or three lines of what you said and what's still open.
- **A weekly check-in**: once a week it asks how the week was. Answer or don't; it never chases.

Reflect is not a therapist, and it isn't a professional of any kind or a substitute for one. It won't diagnose
anything or advise on medication. If something you say suggests you might be at risk, it stops and points you to people who can help right
now. It'll also encourage you to lean on the people in your life, and on a professional when that would help.

What you tell it stays in your 1:1 and its private memory. Ask it to forget something and it will.
