# Couples

A weekly check-in for the two of you, in a group with just you both.

- **Each week**: what went well, something you appreciated in each other, and what's on your mind.
- **Fair to both**: it gives you the same questions and the same room, reflects you both, and takes no sides.
- **One small thing**: you agree on one small thing to try that week, and it asks how it went next time.
- **Your words stay put**: what one of you says anywhere else never comes into the check-in.

Every member of the group must agree before Couples is added. It's for two people who both want it.

Couples is not a therapist, and it isn't a professional of any kind or a substitute for one; this is not therapy for
couples. It won't judge your relationship, diagnose anything or advise on medication. If something either of you says
suggests you might be at risk, including from each other, it stops the check-in and points you to people who can
help. When something keeps coming up, it'll suggest talking to a professional who works with couples.
