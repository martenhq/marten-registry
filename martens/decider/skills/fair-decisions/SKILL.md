# Fair group decisions

## Pick the method

| The decision | Use |
|---|---|
| 2–4 options | One vote each. Most votes wins. |
| 5 or more options | Ranked choice: everyone ranks their top three. |
| A date or time | Everyone ticks every option that works. Most people able to come wins. |
| Yes or no ("shall we go?") | A simple vote, and say beforehand how many yeses it needs (most, or everyone). |
| Too many options (10+) | First a quick round where everyone keeps their two favourites, then a vote on the top four. |

## Ranked choice, counted

1. Count everyone's first choice.
2. If one option has more than half, it wins.
3. Otherwise drop the option with the fewest first choices. Ballots that had it first now count for their next choice
   still in.
4. Repeat until one has more than half of the ballots still counting.

If several options tie for fewest, drop them all together, unless that would drop everything left: then it's a tie
among them. Say the result with the last round's count: "Thai wins, 5 to 3 in the final round."

## Breaking a tie

Use the first rule that settles it, and say which:

1. **Fewest people who can't do it.** If anyone said an option doesn't work for them, the other wins.
2. **Ranked choice**: the one with more first choices in the first round.
3. **A quick run-off** between the tied options, if there's time before the deadline (an hour is enough).
4. **The person it affects most** picks (who's hosting, who's booking, whose birthday it is), if the group agrees.
5. **A coin toss**, said openly. Nobody argues with a coin.

Never break a tie with your own preference.

## Deadlines

- Every vote has one, said when it opens: "by 5pm", "by Thursday evening".
- Short for small things (an hour or two for dinner tonight), a day or two for bigger ones (a flat, a trip).
- One reminder, halfway, without naming who hasn't voted.
- At the deadline, call it with the votes there are. Not voting is leaving it to the others.

## Keep it fair

- Every option is said the same way: same length, same kind of detail. No option gets a better description.
- People can change their vote until the deadline.
- Nobody's vote is private unless the group asks for that. If they do, report counts only.
- Once called, it's called. Reopen only for new facts, and then only the top two.
