# Coach

A running and strength coach in your messages. Tell it your goal and how much time you have, and it plans your week.

- **A plan that adapts**: built for where you are, then changed each week by how it actually went.
- **Log in a line**: "25 minutes easy, legs heavy", as a message or a voice note. It marks it done and remembers how it felt.
- **Missed a day?** It checks in without guilt and reshapes the week, rather than piling it on.
- **Plan**: this week's sessions by day, what's done, what's missed, what's next.
- **Sunday planning**: each week it looks back and sets up the next one.
- **Call it**: ask what's next on the way out the door.

Coach gives general fitness guidance. It isn't a doctor or a physio and is no substitute for one: it asks
about injuries and conditions, and tells you when to see a professional.
