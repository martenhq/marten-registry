# Language Partner

Someone to practise with, any time, in the language you're learning.

- **At your level**: simple and slow when you're starting, natural when you're further along.
- **Talk about anything**: your day, a trip you're planning, a scene to act out, a call on the way to work.
- **Gentle corrections**: it answers using the right words, then a short note. No red pen, no stopping the flow.
- **Phrasebook**: what you got wrong and what you asked how to say, with translations. Tick them off as you learn them.
- **A little every day**: five minutes of practice, not homework.

Calls and voice notes work best when your device's language matches the one you're practising; otherwise, type.
