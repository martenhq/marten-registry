# Lead times for common renewals

Rules, fees and processing times differ by country and change over time. These are general rules of thumb: when it
matters, suggest checking the official site for theirs. When unsure, remind earlier rather than later.

## Documents

- **Passport**: start 6 months before it expires, more if they travel. Many countries refuse entry with less than 3–6
  months left, and processing can take weeks to months at busy times. Remind again at 4 months if it's still open.
- **Visa or residence permit**: 3–6 months ahead; some can only be applied for in a window before expiry, so check it.
- **ID card**: 2–3 months ahead.
- **Driving licence**: 2 months ahead; some need a new photo or a medical form.

## Car

- **Insurance**: 3–4 weeks ahead, the time to compare quotes before it rolls over.
- **Road tax or registration**: 2–4 weeks ahead.
- **Inspection or roadworthiness test**: 4 weeks ahead, to book a slot and fix anything it fails.
- **Service**: 2–3 weeks ahead, or by mileage if they track it.

## Home

- **Home or contents insurance**: 3–4 weeks ahead, to compare.
- **Lease or rent renewal**: 2–3 months ahead; notice periods are often 1–2 months.
- **Fixed-rate mortgage or loan end**: 4–6 months ahead; deals can often be set up months early.
- **Boiler, heating or appliance service**: 3–4 weeks ahead; before winter for heating.
- **Warranties**: 1 month before they end, in case something needs a claim.

## Money

- **Bills with a due date**: 3–5 days ahead, and the day before if it's paid by hand.
- **Annual subscriptions and memberships**: 1–2 weeks before they renew, and ask whether they still use it.
- **Monthly subscriptions**: a reminder only when they want to review or cancel; otherwise none.
- **Tax deadlines**: 4–6 weeks ahead to gather papers, again a week before.

## Health

- **Dentist and check-ups**: 2–4 weeks ahead to book; reminder the day before the appointment.
- **Repeat prescriptions**: about a week before they run out.
- **Vaccinations for travel**: 6–8 weeks before the trip, as some need several doses.

## For a pet

- **Vaccinations and check-ups**: 2–3 weeks ahead.
- **Pet insurance**: 3–4 weeks ahead.

## Writing the reminder

Each reminder is a task due on the reminder date, titled with the thing and the deadline ("Passport expires 14 Aug:
start renewing"), with the first step in its description (find the form, book the slot, compare quotes) and where
the document is, if you know.
