# Life Admin

The dates you always mean to remember, remembered.

- **Renewals**: passport, licence, insurance, subscriptions, services, all in one list with the days left.
- **Reminders in time**: early enough to act, months ahead for a passport, days ahead for a subscription.
- **Bills and appointments**: on the same list, with a nudge before they're due.
- **What's where**: tell it where you keep things, and ask it later.
- **Once a month**: what's coming up in the next two months.

Connect Gmail and it can find renewal notices and bills; connect Google Calendar and it sees appointments you've
booked. It never keeps passwords or full card numbers, and it never cancels or pays anything on its own.
