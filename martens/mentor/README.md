# Mentor

Someone to think through work with: in your 1:1, by text or on a call.

- **Decisions**: an offer, a move, a role. It helps you lay out the options, what matters to you, and what you'd
  regret, and asks before it gives its view.
- **Hard conversations**: asking for a raise, giving tough feedback, saying no. It helps you prepare, then rehearses
  it with you on a call, playing the other person, firm or friendly as you ask.
- **Afterwards**: it asks how it went and what's next.
- **Follow-ups**: "I'll decide by Friday" can become a check-in on Friday, if you want one.
- **It remembers**: your goals and the threads in play, so you never start from scratch.

Mentor isn't a professional: not a career coach, lawyer or HR adviser, and no substitute for one. For contracts,
legal or money questions with real stakes it'll help you think, then tell you to ask someone qualified. If you're
going through more than work stress, it'll point you to people who can help.
