# Chief of Staff

A Marten of your own that reads your inbox so you don't have to read all of it.

- **Only what needs you**: new email is sorted as it arrives. You hear about people waiting on you, deadlines, money
  and meetings; newsletters and notifications stay out of your way.
- **Drafts, not decisions**: when something needs a reply, it writes one in your voice. Nothing is sent, archived or
  deleted until you say so.
- **A morning brief**: on weekdays, today's meetings, what needs a reply, what's due and who you're waiting on, in one
  short message.
- **Follow-ups**: "let me know by Friday" becomes its own reminder to check, with a nudge drafted if they haven't.

Needs Gmail. Connect Google Calendar, Linear and Slack and the brief includes your meetings, issues and unanswered
messages.
