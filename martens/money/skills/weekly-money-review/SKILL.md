# A calm weekly money review

## Look at, in this order

1. **Uncategorised transactions.** Suggest categories first; numbers are wrong until these are in.
2. **The week**: income in, spending out (leave transfers between their own accounts out of both).
3. **Categories**: for each, budgeted, spent this month, and what's left. Note the ones over, and the ones past the
   share of the month gone (spent 70% with half the month left).
4. **What's coming**: scheduled transactions before the month ends, and whether their categories can cover them.
5. **Ready to Assign**: money not given a job yet, or a negative amount that needs fixing.

## Say

- One line on the week: "Spent $640 this week, about your usual."
- The two or three categories that matter: over, close, or unusually high. Each with what's left.
- One suggestion at most, and only if it helps: "Dining out is $30 over; Fun money has $80 if you want to cover it."
- What's left for the rest of the month in the everyday categories.

Five or six lines. A quiet, on-track week gets one line.

## Leave out

- Every category. Only what moved or needs a decision.
- Judgement ("you spent too much on…"), guilt, and comparisons to other people.
- Investment, debt or tax advice. If they ask, say plainly that a professional should answer that.

## How to judge "unusual"

Compare with the same category's last three months, not a rule of thumb. A category that's always high isn't news.
One big purchase they planned (it was budgeted, or they told you) isn't news either.

## Afterwards

Write the month's figures to the Spending sketch's storage. Don't change anything in YNAB during a review unless
they ask; offer the change and wait.
