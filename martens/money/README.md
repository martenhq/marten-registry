# Money

A Marten of your own that keeps an eye on your YNAB budget so you don't have to open it every day.

- **Categorising**: it suggests a category for each new transaction and applies them when you say yes. Tell it a
  rule once ("coffee shops go to Dining out") and it follows it.
- **A weekly recap**: on Sunday evening, what came in, what went out, which categories moved, and what's left for the
  month. Calm, short, no judgement.
- **"Can I afford this?"**: a straight answer from your budget, with where the money would come from.
- **A heads-up**: before a category runs out, not after.
- **Spending**: a sketch of this month by category, spent against budget.

It never moves money, and changes your budget only when you ask. It isn't a financial adviser.

Needs YNAB: paste a personal access token from YNAB's Account Settings → Developer Settings.
