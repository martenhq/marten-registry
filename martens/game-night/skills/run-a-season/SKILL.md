# Running a fair season

## Season points

Different games have different numbers of players, and people miss weeks. Count what you beat, not what you won:

- In each game, a player gets **one point for every player who finished below them**. Winning a five-player game is
  4 points; winning a two-player game is 1; last place is 0.
- Tied players share their place and score the same: in 1, 2, 2, 4 the two seconds each get 1 point (they beat only
  the fourth).
- A game's own score (quiz points, poker chips, victory points) decides the order in that game. It isn't added to
  the season.

This keeps a two-player game from counting as much as a full table, and doesn't punish anyone for a big game.

## The table

Sort by season points, then wins, then fewer games played (doing as much in fewer games is better), then name. Show
games played next to points so a missed week is visible, not hidden.

If attendance varies a lot, also show points per game, and say which the group ranks by. Ask the group which they
want before the season starts, not halfway through.

## Odd results

- **Team games**: everyone on the winning team shares first place; the other team shares second.
- **Co-op games** (everyone wins or loses together): record them, but they score no season points.
- **Someone leaves early**: they finish last among those still playing when they left, unless the table says
  otherwise.
- **A guest**: record them by name. They count in the game, so beating them earns points; they're in the table too,
  and the group can choose to hide guests from it.
- **A replayed or disputed game**: keep the result the players agree on. Never count both.

## Seasons

- Agree the length up front: a number of weeks or games (8 to 12 weeks works for most groups).
- A week before the end, post the table and what each of the top few needs.
- At the end, post the final table and the champion, then start the next season with everyone at zero. Past seasons
  stay in the Scoreboard.
- Changes to the rules apply from the next season, unless every regular agrees.
